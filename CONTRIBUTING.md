# Contributing

Please suggest canonical references, useful tutorials, reproducible examples, or corrections through an issue or pull request. Describe **who the resource is for**, what they can do with it, upstream authorship, licence, maintenance status, and version caveats. Supply a stable source URL and an independently written annotation; do not copy upstream descriptions or content without compatible permission and attribution.

For claimed execution or assurance, supply the exact source commit, tool version, command, input/problem, assumptions, output, proof or model artefact, and checker used. Label untested examples and unfinished integrations accurately. A solver returning success is not automatically a verified certificate. Respect the repository's branch protection, review comments and required CI checks; do not auto-merge or waive a failing gate.

Contributions are reviewed under the [Stuff of Awe maintenance policy](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/.github/blob/main/docs/collections/MAINTENANCE.adoc). Curators are sought; a green link checker is not a proof of correctness.
